# Online Bookstore Data Analysis | MySQL

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing an Online Bookstore database using MySQL. The objective is to explore book inventory, customer information, and order transactions to answer business-related questions and extract meaningful insights.

## Database Structure
The project consists of three relational tables:

- **Books:** Contains book details such as title, author, genre, published year, price, and stock.
- **Customers:** Contains customer information, including name, email, phone number, city, and country.
- **Orders:** Contains order details, including customer ID, book ID, order date, quantity, and total amount.

## Business Questions Solved

### Basic Analysis
1. Retrieve books belonging to the Fiction genre.
2. Find books published after 1950.
3. Identify customers from Canada.
4. Retrieve orders placed in November 2023.
5. Calculate the total stock of available books.
6. Find the most expensive book.
7. Identify customers who ordered more than one quantity of a book.
8. Retrieve orders with a total amount exceeding $20.
9. Identify all available book genres.
10. Find the book with the lowest stock.
11. Calculate total revenue generated from orders.

### Advanced Analysis
1. Calculate the total number of books sold for each genre.
2. Find the average price of Fantasy books.
3. Identify customers who have placed at least two orders.
4. Find the most frequently ordered book.
5. Retrieve the top three most expensive Fantasy books.
6. Calculate the total quantity of books sold by each author.
7. Identify cities where customers placed orders worth more than $30.
8. Find the customer who spent the most on orders.
9. Calculate the remaining stock of each book after fulfilling orders.
